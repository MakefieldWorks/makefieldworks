Rules
DO
  • Use the product's shipping mark as-is. Don't redraw it to "match" the parent.
  • Put the endorsement in the footer: a Makefield Works project.
  • Keep at least the mark's width of padding around it on any surface.
  • Flip outline colors for dark backgrounds — the parent mark should invert cleanly.
DON'T
  • Don't lock up the parent mark next to a product mark. Keep them on separate surfaces.
  • Don't recolor a product mark to the parent blue.
  • Don't stretch, rotate, or outline any mark.
  • Don't put a product mark on a colored field other than white, slate-950, or its listed tinted background.
