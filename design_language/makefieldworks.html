02 — Approach

Three things every Makefield project has in common.

01 · Local-first

Your data stays on your device.

No accounts, no uploads, no servers holding copies of your salary or your route. Planning inputs live in your browser or on your machine, and stay there.

02 · Built from use

Each tool began as a personal need.

FERSCalc started as a spreadsheet for a real retirement decision. VPP was built to plan a real passage. The tools exist because they were needed, not because they filled a market gap slide.

03 · Quiet by design

No hype, no upsell, no dashboard theater.

Makefield projects try to give you a clearer picture of a decision and then get out of the way. If a product doesn't need a feature, it doesn't get one.

Software shouldn't require you to trade your data for a clearer picture of your own life.
— The working principle
