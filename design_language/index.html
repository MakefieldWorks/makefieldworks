A quiet system for four careful tools.

The shared design DNA behind Makefield Works and its family of products — FERSCalc, Visual Passage Planner, Manifest, and The Grunting Gardener. Derived from FERSCalc's existing tokens so adoption cost is near zero. Every product keeps its own name, its own accent, and the footer line: a Makefield Works project.
