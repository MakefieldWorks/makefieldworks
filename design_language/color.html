Color.
One neutral, one brand, three accents. Color earns its place — if a surface can be slate, it should be. Saturated color is reserved for meaning (trust, caution, warning) and for per-product identity.
Slate · neutral
The workhorse. 95% of UI — backgrounds, text, rules, dividers, chips — is slate. Tailwind's canonical slate scale (50–950). Mirrors FERSCalc exactly.

Status accents
Used for meaning, not decoration. Each accent has a narrow, defined job.
emerald-600 · #059669
Trust
Free / no-account / local-first pills. "Data stays in your browser" badges. Positive deltas in finance readouts. Never used as a primary action color.
amber-500 · #f59e0b
Caution
Stress-test badges. Monte Carlo probability-of-depletion. "Planning approximation" labels on coverage gaps. Not used for errors.
rose-500 · #f43f5e
Warning · Destructive
Validation errors. Negative deltas. Delete-scenario confirmations. Reserved — overusing rose makes products feel angry.
paper · #fafaf7 parent-only
Paper (parent brand)
Warm off-white background used only on makefieldworks.com and design-system docs. Products themselves stay white / slate-950. Signals "editorial frame around the workshop."
Per-product accents
Each product picks one accent from the brand or emerald scales. The accent shows up on the wordmark dot, active navigation state, and one-off moments — not on every button.

Rules
DO
  • Use slate for almost everything. Default to slate-200 for rules, slate-500–700 for body, slate-950 for headlines.
  • Use brand-700 for primary buttons, links, and interactive focus.
  • Use emerald only where it carries meaning — trust, positive deltas, "free" pills.
  • Support a .dark class on html. Flip background to slate-950, ink to slate-50–300.
  • Keep contrast at WCAG AA or better for all body text.
DON'T
  • Don't use gradients. No gradient backgrounds, no gradient text. One color per surface.
  • Don't introduce new hues per product — pick from the scales.
  • Don't use rose for anything non-destructive — it reads as "error," not "accent."
  • Don't put paper (#fafaf7) inside products. Paper is the parent-brand frame.
  • Don't stack two accent colors side-by-side in one card — pick one.
