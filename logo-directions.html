Brand mark locked

The Makefield Works mark is now set.

Makefield Works needs a mark that can sit behind retirement planning, sailing software, operations tooling, and future products without dragging the family toward any one category. The chosen mark stays geometric, restrained, and durable.

Locked decision

Workshop Frame is the official Makefield Works mark. It is abstract enough to travel, distinct at favicon size, and restrained enough to stay in the background behind the product brands.

Direction 01

Locked

Workshop Frame

A piece of work held inside a workshop boundary. Simple geometry, one outer frame, one inner plate. It feels like a maker’s imprint without becoming rustic or literal.

  • Best favicon and header mark
  • Feels stable across very different products
  • Already present in your current site direction

Direction 02

Confluence Grid

Two crossing axes inside a rounded frame. This is the most place-based option, a quiet nod to river, canal, and crossroads without turning the logo into landscape illustration.

  • Useful geographic subtext
  • More diagram-like than brand-like
  • Archived alternate, not selected

Direction 03

Maker's Stamp

A monogram-like square mark built from the initials. It feels more proprietary and more traditional. Cleaner than many lettermarks, but less neutral than the frame.

  • Most brand-forward option
  • Harder to read at tiny sizes
  • Archived alternate, not selected

Specimen

How the recommended mark behaves.

The strongest version is still the simple one: icon first, quiet wordmark, no extra decoration. It can live in the site header, favicon, or footer endorsement without stealing attention from the product names.

Dark surface

It also holds up on slate.

This matters because you may want it on software splash screens, docs, or repository art later. The frame remains crisp when reversed without adding a second accent color.

Decision

What is locked.

Direction 01 is locked. It is the most flexible and least likely to age badly. It says “workshop” and “container” without saying finance, sailing, gardening, or enterprise software.

Directions 02 and 03 are kept as historical alternates only. They are useful contrast, but Direction 01 has the right amount of identity for an umbrella brand whose job is endorsement, not attention capture.
